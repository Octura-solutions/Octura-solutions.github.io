How much does an Odoo implementation cost?

Most projects range from $15K to $80K depending on module count, users, and customization depth. You get a fixed-price estimate after a free 30-minute discovery call — no T&M surprises. See our transparent Odoo pricing, the honest cost of Odoo in 2026, or try the implementation cost calculator.

How long until we go live?

8 to 12 weeks for a standard rollout. Multi-company, manufacturing, or heavy-custom scopes run 4 to 6 months. We publish the timeline on day one and hit it on 95% of projects. See our realistic Odoo implementation timeline, the 14-step playbook, and the 12 milestones every owner should track.

Can Odoo be customized to fit our unique processes?

Yes — but we push back when customization isn't worth it. Odoo's 60+ native modules cover roughly 80% of most businesses out of the box. See our custom Odoo development service, hire a senior Odoo developer, or read why standard Odoo is your secret to long-term scalability.

Can you migrate data from our current system?

We've migrated clients off SAP, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Zoho, Sage, and Excel without data loss. See the full data migration service, our migration cost calculator, and the 10 mistakes to avoid during data migration.

Does Odoo integrate with the tools we already use?

Out of the box: Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, Stripe, PayPal, FedEx/UPS/DHL, Mailchimp, QuickBooks, Salesforce, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, WhatsApp, and 100+ more. See integrations, the Shopify + Stripe + Odoo guide, and our Odoo 19 REST API guide.

What's the difference between Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Community is free and open-source but missing accounting, Studio, IoT, and mobile app support. Enterprise costs roughly $31/user/month and unlocks the full stack. Read the Community vs. Enterprise vs. Studio comparison and the Community-to-Enterprise migration guide.

What support do you provide after go-live?

95% of our clients stay on a support plan. Dedicated architect, 4-hour response SLA, bug fixes, performance tuning, user training refreshers, and new feature development. See support plans, our technical support policy, and the first-90-days post go-live guide.
